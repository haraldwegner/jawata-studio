## One cause behind "empty" (Memory, Reporting, Settings)

`.settings-section` is `height: 100%`, and its inner `.stack` is a grid with no `align-content: start`. The grid therefore shares the window's height out among its rows, and a closed fold is drawn as a tall empty box. Sizing the rows to their content removes the empty space on all three tabs. This is a layout fix, not new content.
