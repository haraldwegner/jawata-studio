# v3.15.0 — the two products carry one version again

A version alignment, not a feature release: the studio moves from 3.14.x to **3.15.0**
so it names the same generation as the engine it drives (jawata-mcp v3.15.x). Nothing
in the studio's behaviour changes from v3.14.3.

Why the numbers had drifted: the engine took a minor bump for two schema additions —
**tombstones** (v14: what a rebuild of the knowledge store deliberately removes stays
removed, on every machine, however often the deploy-time auto-import runs) and the
**cause field** (v15: the diagnosis a piece of knowledge addresses, first-class and
queryable) — while the studio only carried the instruction-side half of the same work.
Two products at two generation numbers, doing one thing, is a reading hazard every time
someone asks which version has what.

What shipped in v3.14.3 and rides along unchanged:

- `/memorize` and `/review` teach and check the story structure **Situation →
  Complication → Solution, and whether it worked**.
- The autocontinue loop stands down when the human is present: a window opened by
  their own question is never pushed into new work, an interrupt ends the grant, and a
  decision ask is detected however it is phrased.
