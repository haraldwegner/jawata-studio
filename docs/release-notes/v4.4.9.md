# jawata-studio v4.4.9

**A running workspace cannot be deleted, every delete asks first, project paths are back, and the tray and About name both versions.**

## What went wrong

These were found in a walkthrough of 4.4.8:

- **A workspace could be deleted while its service ran,** and deleting from the Dashboard card took effect without a question.
- **The Dashboard's More menu held only Delete all, and the button did nothing.**
- **Project folder paths were gone from the cards,** although they are what you set a workspace up by.
- **Choosing a `.code-workspace` file folded the Add projects panel shut** in the middle of the import.
- **Memory's buttons had different sizes,** the result box was called "Result", and the store's contents started folded.
- **Reporting and Settings looked empty:** rows stretched down the whole window and Details started folded.
- **Help was short** and said little about building a workspace.

## What changes

- **A workspace whose projects are running or starting cannot be deleted, and neither can one of its projects.** The rule lives in the backend, so no path reaches the deletion while the service is live, and the screen greys its controls. A test covers the mixed case of one project running beside one stopped.
- **Every delete asks in a pop-up that names what it removes.** Deleting a workspace's last project says that the workspace goes too. Source files on disk are never deleted.
- **Delete all workspaces is at the bottom of the Workspaces panel,** and the Dashboard's More menu is gone.
- **Each project card shows its folder path again.**
- **The Add projects panel stays open while you import.**
- **Memory:** the four actions are the same size, the result box is called Status, and "What the store holds" is open.
- **Reporting:** Details is open. On every tab, rows sit at the top instead of stretching down the window.
- **The tray menu and About show the studio and engine versions.**
- **Help is rebuilt around building a workspace,** in five steps.

## Known limits

- **The screen changes were checked by tests and by reading the code, not by looking at a rendered window.** The delete rules are tested.
- **Help has no screenshots yet;** they come at the end of the sprint.
- **Whether a workspace is live is worked out on the screen and in the backend separately.** The two agree today, and giving the fact one place is filed for Sprint 28h.

Works with jawata-mcp 4.4.14.
