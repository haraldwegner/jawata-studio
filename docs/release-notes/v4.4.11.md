# jawata-studio v4.4.11

**The Memory, Reporting, Seats, Dashboard, Settings and Help views lose what repeated or served no decision, and three display defects from 4.4.10 are fixed.**

## What went wrong

- **A space was lost beside a figure** inside a template block (Memory: "189of 11808 rows", "describedin"; Reporting: "answering(62 ms)").
- **The Reporting fold "Residents not answering" listed every resident,** including the ones that answer.
- **The Add projects placeholders were cut** ("/a/java/project, or a fol").
- **Memory drew its folds before the store was read,** so two folds became three and the page jumped; the store's figures sat in a fold that read as closed.
- **Reporting opened on Details,** listed 3 failure shapes plus "5 more" of 72 without saying the list was cut, and showed six recall figures with no decision behind them.
- **On Seats the fold sign and the name sat halfway down a two-line row,** and one name pushed its sentence out of line.

## What changes

- **Tray:** the health of your services is in the Workspaces line, and the second line reads "jawata studio X / engine Y".
- **Memory:** nothing but "Reading the store…" is drawn until the read ends. The figures ("What the store holds") are plain content, not a fold. The store table drops the Entries and Size columns the top line already gives, the pattern library line drops its repeated row count, and "What the cataloguer wrote" (ten sampled engine members) is gone; the Cataloguer line keeps the counts.
- **Reporting:** opens on the sentence and one line of totals (failure shapes recorded, filed, still to report); Details is closed. Details lists every failure shape, most repeated first, and the six recall figures are one sentence, with what a shape is and what "worth reporting" means said in words.
- **Seats:** the fold sign and name sit level with the first line of the sentence; every sentence starts at one edge.
- **Dashboard:** the Workspaces paragraph is a mouseover; the Managed Projects line shows only how many workspaces run (the counts are in the header); a workspace card shows one button, Stop while it runs and Start while it is stopped; Deploy to agents and Remove from agents are two ordinary buttons, not a two-segment switch.
- **Settings:** the opening sentence says only what Settings decides (a newer engine, and how many agents Deploy writes to). Release source, Global JAWATA Source, the custom repo and the local jar are under Engine → Advanced; Update policy says what each choice does; the link to the studio's releases appears once, beside it. Paths drops the workspace and project counts, and Clear sits in one row with Browse.
- **Help:** each section above is changed with its view; the agent config-file table (Settings shows the real paths), the install commands (README) and the subtitle are gone.

## Known limits

- **Help has no screenshots yet;** they are made after this layout is settled.
- **The Dashboard's project rows, the About dialog's version lines and the tray menu were not rendered** (a static render cannot show the native tray menu or whether a panel scrolls); your look at the installed window decides them.
- **The failure-shape totals count a shape once per workspace,** the way the "still to report" figure always has.

Works with jawata-mcp 4.4.15.
