# jawata-studio v3.14.1

Companion to jawata v3.14.1 (the dogfood patch). One change of substance.

## The review seat gains the quality lane

`/review` now answers three questions instead of two: what does nobody use
(the deletion list), what is asked for and missing (the writing backlog), and —
new — **what is badly written** (the quality lane, from the engine's new store
repair surface).

The rewrite step is propose-first, like the delete step always was: the seat
reads the entry, drafts the situation as a real condition, shows the current
text and the draft side by side, and calls `set_form` only on your yes. A
finding that carries a `source_ref` is durably fixed in that file and reseeded —
a store write there is erased by the next reseed; a finding without one has no
file behind it, and the store write is the durable fix. Every rewrite is
stamped `seat_rewritten`, so the seat's corrections are always distinguishable
from your own words.
